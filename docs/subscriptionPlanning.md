## Features That Should Stay Free

These features should remain available to every Ascendra user:

- Tasks and habits
- Journal
- Calendar
- Basic statistics
- Basic Ascendra AI
- Unwind
- Study Space
- Dark mode
- Account syncing between a limited number of devices

This prevents free users from being hit by the **Paywall of Doom™**.

## Recommended First Version

The first version of Ascendra Pro could include:

1. Advanced statistics
2. Premium themes
3. AI weekly reports
4. Unlimited time capsules
5. Custom soundscapes
6. Automatic backups
7. Exclusive Ascendra AI customizations

Starting with a smaller set of features would make Pro exciting without requiring too many complicated backend systems immediately.

## Pricing Options

Ascendra Elevate could be offered as:

- A monthly subscription
- A yearly subscription
- A one-time lifetime purchase
- Both a subscription and lifetime option

Ascendra Elevate Pricing

Ascendra Elevate uses localized pricing based on the user’s country.

For example, users in Canada will see:

$.20 CAD/week
$1 CAD/month
$9 CAD/year
$20 CAD lifetime
14-day free trial
A reminder 3 days before the free trial ends
Cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged

After the trial ends, the subscription will automatically renew for $1 CAD/month unless cancelled.
