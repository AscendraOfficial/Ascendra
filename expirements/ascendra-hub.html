Ascendra - Tasks, Habits and Goals

The Ultimate Productivity Website

Ascendra is a productivity and personal development web application designed to help users organise their lives, build positive habits, and achieve personal goals. It combines tools such as task management, calendar planning, habit tracking, journaling, breathing exercises, and focused study sessions within a clean, user-friendly interface. Through gamification features such as XP, levels, achievements, and rewards, Ascendra encourages consistency and makes everyday progress more engaging. Its ultimate goal is to bring productivity, wellbeing, and personal growth together in one cohesive platform.

Created by Declan Chong, Jayden Wang and Natalie Wang
