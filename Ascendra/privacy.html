Your privacy matters

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Ascendra collects, stores, uses, and protects your information.

01

Information We Collect

Ascendra stores information that you choose to enter into the application, including:

Calendar events
Journal entries
To-do lists
Habits
App settings
02

How Your Information Is Stored

Currently, Ascendra stores your information locally on your device using your web browser's Local Storage. Your information is not sent to Ascendra servers.

Journal Security

Journal entries can be protected with additional password-based encryption. When enabled, journal information is encrypted before it is stored on your device.

03

Data Sharing

Ascendra does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

04

Cookies

Ascendra does not currently use cookies for tracking or advertising.

05

Security

We work to keep your information safe. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Important

While Ascendra uses security measures to protect your information, users should understand that software can contain bugs or vulnerabilities.

06

Your Control

You may delete your stored information at any time by clearing your browser data or using features within Ascendra that remove saved information.

07

Future Updates

If Ascendra adds cloud sync, user accounts, or additional online features, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.
